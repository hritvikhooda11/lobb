Support

How can we help?

Email support@getlobb.com. We reply within 24 hours, usually much faster.

Problem with an order

Open the order in the app and tap Report it, or use Account → Report a problem. If you were charged for something you didn’t get, we’ll refund you.

Report or block someone

Tap the flag in a chat, on a shout-out, or on an order. You can block them right there. Manage blocked people in Account → Blocked people. We review every report within 24 hours.

Payments and payouts

Your card is held when you order and charged only when you get the item. Neighbors get paid through Stripe: set it up in Account → Payouts.

Delete your account

In the app: Account → Delete account. It removes your profile, listings, shout-outs, chats and photos right away. Can’t sign in? Email us from your UW address and we’ll delete it for you.

Who can join

UW students 18 and older living in a building Lobb supports. Sign in with your @uw.edu email; we send you a code, no password.

What can be sold

New, factory-sealed, shelf-stable items, and non-food items to lend. Never alcohol, tobacco or vapes, cannabis, supplements, medication or weapons.
