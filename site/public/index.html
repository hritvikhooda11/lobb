How it works

Order from your building, not a warehouse.

1

Pick it

See what neighbors in your building have right now, or shout out for anything that isn’t listed.

2

A neighbor brings it

Door drop-off, lobby meetup, or pickup. Chat with them and get a heads-up when they’re there.

3

Scan and done

Show your QR code at the handoff. Your card is only charged when the item is in your hands.

Two ways to use Lobb

Need something? Have something?

When you need it

  • Sealed snacks, drinks, instant food and toiletries
  • Borrow chargers, calculators, umbrellas and more
  • Prices set from real store prices
  • Only verified UW students in your building

When you have it

Earn on every handoff
  • List what’s already in your room in seconds: scan a barcode or snap a photo
  • Get paid back plus a delivery fee, straight to your bank
  • Lend things out and get them back, with a deposit hold
